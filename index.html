Who I Train

You already show up.
Now let's make it count.

Private coaching for adults who are already active and want more — better mechanics, smarter programming, and a trainer who brings a clinical eye to every session.

The Intentional Lifter

You show up consistently but want someone to confirm your form, correct your mechanics, and build a plan that matches your actual goals — not a generic routine.

Managing Pain or an Old Injury

A knee, hip, or shoulder that keeps limiting you. You want to stay active and get stronger with someone who understands anatomy and won't make it worse.

Ready to Level Up

Consistent but plateaued. You want progressive, structured training that challenges you and tracks exactly where you're going.

Active with a Health Condition

Managing a diagnosis or medications — and you want a trainer whose clinical background means you can push without second-guessing your safety.

About Gretchen

I don't just teach it.
I live it.

At 54, I'm on zero medications and training for triathlons every season. I swim, bike, run, lift, and show up to every session having done exactly what I'm asking you to do.

Eight years as an ICU nurse taught me what happens when people stop moving — and what's possible when they don't. I brought that clinical eye into my training practice because it makes every session safer, smarter, and more effective.

I notice what other trainers miss — compensation patterns, pain signals, how medications affect performance. And I adjust in real time.
